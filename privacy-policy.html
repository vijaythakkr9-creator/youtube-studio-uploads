Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 November 2026

This policy covers Thakkar Studio Uploader (formerly "Studio Uploads"), a private desktop tool used only by its owner, Vijay Thakkar, to manage the owner's own YouTube channels: Then What, Really?, Agar Sach Mein. and Everyday, Opened. It is not offered to the public, and no other person can sign in to it.

What it accesses

With the owner's permission, the tool uses YouTube API Services to:

  • upload and schedule the owner's own videos, with their titles, descriptions, thumbnails and caption tracks;
  • add those videos to the owner's own playlists and post one pinned comment on the owner's own videos;
  • read the owner's own channels' public statistics and YouTube Analytics reports (read-only), to plan better videos.

It only accesses the owner's own channels. It does not access other users' accounts, and it does not download, copy or re-upload anyone else's content.

What it stores

Google sign-in tokens and the analytics numbers it reads are stored only on the owner's own computer. The tool does not collect personal data about viewers or anyone else.

What it shares

The tool does not sell or share data with anyone. Content the owner uploads is sent to YouTube. The tool's use of YouTube API Services is subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Removing access

The owner can remove the tool's access at any time from their Google Account permissions page (security settings). Stored tokens are then deleted from the owner's computer.
