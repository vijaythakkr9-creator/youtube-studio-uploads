Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 November 2026

This policy covers YouTube Studio Uploads, a private tool used only by its owner to manage the owner's own YouTube channels.

What it accesses

With the owner's permission, the tool uses the YouTube Data API to upload videos, thumbnails and captions, and to confirm which channel it is connected to. It only accesses the owner's own channels.

What it stores

Google login tokens are stored only on the owner's computer. The tool does not collect data about viewers or anyone else.

What it shares

The tool does not sell or share data. Content the owner uploads is sent to YouTube. The tool's use of YouTube API Services is subject to the Google Privacy Policy.

Removing access

The owner can remove the tool's access at any time from their Google Account permissions page.

Contact

Questions can be raised through the project's issue page.
